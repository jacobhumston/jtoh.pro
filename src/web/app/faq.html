Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

The image is outdated when sent on Discord, what's wrong?

Discord saves (or technically known as "caches") the image when it is first sent. This means that if the same exact URL is sent again, Discord will display the cached image for a while instead of fetching a new one. To prevent this from happening:
- Copy or download the image from the 'Try it out!' section.
- Add a random query string to the end of the URL, such as ?abc1234 .
- Use the "Copy URL" button provided on the 'Try it out!' section, as it automatically adds a nocache query string for you.
If none of the above fixes your problem, wait at least 5 minutes before sending the image again.

Are banned accounts not supported?

They are supported, but stats may be innacurate.

Why is this user's name a different color on the card?

Users that have donated any amount of Robux to towerstats.com will display as yellow on the card. They will also have a star emoji next to their display name. Other colors are specific to other people that I personally know.
I do plan on adding some customization options soon.

Will other fan games be supported?

Yes! I will add fan game support as they are added to towerstats.com . Every fan game will have its own unique URL, if available.
