2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites function properly, to improve user experience, and to provide information to the website operator. Cookies may be classified as:

  • ● Session Cookies, which are deleted when you close your browser.
  • ● Persistent Cookies, which remain on your device until they expire or are deleted.
  • ● First-Party Cookies, which are placed by us.
  • ● Third-Party Cookies, which are set by third-party services integrated with our Website.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use the following categories of cookies on the Services:

  • (a) Essential Cookies. These cookies are necessary for the operation of the Services and cannot be disabled. For example, we use an “auth-login” cookie to maintain your authenticated session after logging in via Roblox or Discord. Without this cookie, you would need to log in again each time you visit the Services.
  • (b) Analytics Cookies. We use Cloudflare Analytics and Google Analytics cookies to collect information about how visitors use the Services. These cookies help us understand usage patterns, measure performance, and improve functionality.
  • (c) Advertising Cookies. We may use cookies through Google AdSense to deliver personalized advertising. These cookies may track your browsing activity across the Services and other sites in order to provide ads tailored to your interests. Google AdSense will not be activated until this Cookie Policy and other legal documents are publicly available on the Website.

7. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies, delete cookies, or be notified before cookies are placed. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impair or disable core functionality of the Services, including the ability to log in and remain authenticated. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, available at: https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
