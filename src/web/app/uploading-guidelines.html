Uploading Guidelines

When uploading custom content such as images, you must follow a few guidelines to avoid having your custom content removed. Note that we reserve the right to remove any content that violates these guidelines or any other content when it is deemed appropriate to do so by us.

Content that violates any of the following items may be subject to removal:

  • Content that is illegal in the United States.
  • Content that is offensive, hateful, or discriminatory.
  • Content that is sexually explicit or sexually suggestive.
  • Content that is intended to harass, bully, or intimidate others.
  • Content that is intended to impersonate another person or entity.
  • Content that is intended to mislead or deceive others.
  • Content that is intended to promote or advertise products or services.
  • Content that is politically motivated.
  • Use of copyrighted content that does not fall under fair use.

We will also remove content that poses a security risk to the site or its users, such as content containing malware, viruses, and alike.

If you have any questions, please contact us!
